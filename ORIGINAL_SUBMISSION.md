## Idea
AI-Driven Deepfake Detection System for Face-Swap Video Authentication

Problem Statement
The increasing accessibility of AI-powered face-swapping tools has led to a rapid rise in deepfake videos, posing serious threats to digital trust, cybersecurity, privacy, and public safety. These manipulated videos can be used to spread misinformation, commit identity fraud, damage reputations, and bypass verification systems. Current detection methods often struggle to keep pace with evolving deepfake generation techniques, creating an urgent need for a robust and intelligent detection framework.

Proposed Solution
We propose an AI/ML-based Deepfake Detection System capable of identifying face-swap manipulated videos with high accuracy and real-time performance. The solution leverages advanced Computer Vision and Deep Learning models to analyze facial landmarks, temporal inconsistencies, eye movements, lip synchronization, lighting variations, skin texture artifacts, and frame-level anomalies.
The system processes uploaded videos and generates an authenticity score, highlighting manipulated regions and providing explainable insights into the detection results. By combining spatial and temporal analysis, the model can detect sophisticated deepfakes that traditional methods may overlook.

Key Features
AI-powered face-swap deepfake detection
Real-time video analysis and verification
Authenticity confidence score
Manipulation heatmap highlighting altered regions
Explainable AI for transparent decision-making
Web and mobile-friendly interface
Scalable API integration for social media, news agencies, and verification platforms

Innovation
Unlike conventional detection systems that focus only on individual frames, our solution combines facial behavior analysis, temporal consistency checks, and deep neural networks to improve detection accuracy against next-generation deepfake technologies.

Expected Impact
Combat misinformation and fake media.
Prevent identity theft and digital impersonation.
Enhance trust in online content and communication.
Support law enforcement and digital forensic investigations.
Strengthen verification systems in banking, education, recruitment, and governance.

Technology Stack
Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Computer Vision, CNNs, Vision Transformers, OpenCV, Python, Flask/FastAPI, and Explainable AI techniques.
